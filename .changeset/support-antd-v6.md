Support Ant Design v6 as a peer dependency (`antd@^5.23.0 || ^6.0.0` and `@ant-design/icons@^5.5.1 || ^6.0.0`).

This resolves peer dependency conflicts when installing or upgrading to Ant Design v6 in Refine projects while maintaining full backward compatibility with Ant Design v5.
